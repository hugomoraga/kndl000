# Dispositivos — atelier

## Scope and mode

- Surface: `/dispositivos/` (`dispositivos/index.md`).
- Mode: Experience. The instruments lead; the interface is a frame.
- Out of scope: the archive rail, the device detail layout, and the site navigation. The mockup's top bar (Archivo, Notas, Sobre) is not this site's nav and was not built.
- Approved comp: the user's mockup of the dispositivos index. Spatial contract, not a license to invent stacks.

## Audience and job

A reader arriving at the laboratory of instruments. They should see what is being built, open the featured piece, and find the rest without reading a spec sheet first. Version and status remain one scroll away.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dispositivos is an atelier wall, not a module rack. The first thing visible is the instrument (a plate), then its name. It refuses the three-column spec grid and the fake site nav in the comp.

OWN-WORLD: Chamber black, Newsreader at a present weight, JetBrains Mono only for counts, numbers and marks. Hairline frames, no radius, no shadow. One cyan trace on the arrow and the frame when the piece is chosen. Plates are grain, line and dust — the same family as the approved comp.

STORY: The visitor reads the count, sees Nacre as the piece in front, then the other five instruments, and can drop into the version archive without leaving the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with a cropped moon at the left of the title, italic lede, mono count, and a narrow aside behind a hairline. Below, a full-width featured frame: particle plate left, Nacre right, arrow at the lower right of the copy. Collection begins in the same viewport if the width allows; on a phone it follows immediately.

FORM: Atelier wall, pinned by the approved comp. seed: none (pinned) "quiero rehacer la sección dispositivos con este mockup".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

# Home — Cámara de niebla

## Scope and mode

- Surface: `/` (`index.md`), the entrance to the archive.
- Mode: Experience. The work leads; the interface recedes.
- Out of scope: every other route, collection template, and shared layout beyond a `body_class` hook.
- Preserved by explicit request: the generative canvas and the laboratory feed.
- Removed by explicit request: the random collage and the terminal line. The six section links survive as navigation and as a closing index.

## Audience and job

Writers, artists, and technical peers arriving without a task. They should recognize a voice in seconds and choose a door into the archive. Success is a first scroll and an outbound click, not a conversion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a cloud chamber: a dark instrument where invisible signals condense into visible traces. It refuses the portfolio grid, the cards, and the hero-metrics arrangement; the archive's own texts and images are the evidence.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black chamber (#060708), silver-white condensation, one cold cyan trace and one amber event. Newsreader for voice, JetBrains Mono only for measurement and archive hashes. Hairline rules at 1px, generous dark, no glows as decoration — only light that a trace actually emits.

STORY: The visitor sees a title cut by a threshold, watches tracks condense out of the dark, understands that text, image, and code are the same kind of event here, then descends into the live laboratory and the six exits.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas chamber. A thin luminous threshold line crosses the title at its own middle, around 40% of the viewport at both inspected sizes. CENIZAS is set in large Newsreader across that line, its upper half silver and lower half dimmed, so the word crosses the membrane. Top bar: KNDL 000 left, Estado umbral right; no menu. Bottom: the six section links in one quiet row (3×2 on phones), plus a single descent mark. No centered headline over subcopy, no cards.

FORM: Cloud chamber as the native instrument of the surface; the threshold is the condensation line. seed: a4cf1939935810e0.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
